FAQ

Good to know

How do I receive my ticket?

After payment you receive a confirmation email from Stripe. Your name is added to the guest list; show the email or simply give your name at the entrance.

Can I buy tickets for other people?

Each payment covers one guest. To bring others, complete one payment per guest using their name and email, so everyone is on the guest list.

What is included in the €70?

Entry, the full buffet, champagne tasting, the live entertainment and the DJ set. €10 of the price is donated to the orphanage.

I have a food allergy. What should I do?

Mention it in the "Food allergies" field when you pay, and we will pass it on to the caterer.

Who can I contact?

Write to alc.luxembourg@gmail.com and the organising team will get back to you.
