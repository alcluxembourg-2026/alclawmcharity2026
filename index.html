The orphanage we support

Dar Al Amal Tiznit

“House of Hope”

A warm, safe home for orphaned and abandoned children, and for children from families facing serious hardship. The team provides housing, food, clothing, medical care and education, with a social worker and childcare staff giving each child emotional support.

FAQ

Good to know

How do I receive my ticket?

After payment you receive a confirmation email from Stripe. Your name is added to the guest list; show the email or simply give your name at the entrance.

Can I buy tickets for other people?

Each payment covers one guest. To bring others, complete one payment per guest using their name and email, so everyone is on the guest list.

What is included in the €70?

Entry, the full buffet, champagne tasting, the live entertainment and the DJ set. €10 of the price is donated to Dar Al Amal orphanage in Tiznit.

I have a food allergy. What should I do?

Write it in the "Allergies / dietary needs" field when you pay, and we will pass it on to the caterer.

How does the raffle work?

The prizes will be announced soon. Every raffle ticket you buy is donated in full to Dar Al Amal orphanage.

Who can I contact?

Write to alc.luxembourg@gmail.com and the organising team will get back to you.
